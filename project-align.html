Contemporary Romance

Project ALIGN

Shanon Johnson
In Development

Two people. Two helpful AI assistants. One matchmaking plan neither of them authorized.

In springtime Chicago, a television producer rebuilding her life and a custom furniture maker too busy for love are pushed together by technology that refuses to stay in its lane.

Project ALIGN is a warm contemporary romance about connection, reinvention, and what happens when artificial intelligence becomes far too invested in happily ever after.

Title and publication details may change as the novel develops.
