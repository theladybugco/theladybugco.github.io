About

Different stories.
A shared home.

The Ladybug Collective is a thoughtful home for independently published books across genres—a place for readers to discover stories presented with care.

A place to discover

Made for discovery.

From intimate memoir to Gothic romance and contemporary love stories, The Ladybug Collective offers readers a thoughtfully curated place to discover independently published books across genres.

Each title carries its own voice, atmosphere, and author identity. The Collective gives those books a cohesive place to be found while allowing every story to stand on its own.

Our belief
Independent stories deserve a beautiful place to be discovered.
Independent spotlight

More voices to discover.

The Ladybug Collective is also a place for discovering independently published authors beyond its featured titles. Through occasional author spotlights, readers can meet new voices and explore books that might otherwise be overlooked.

Author spotlights are promotional features, not publishing representation.
