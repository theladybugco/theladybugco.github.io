About

A home for independent stories.

The Ladybug Collective was created to give independently published books across genres and author identities one cohesive place to be discovered.

What we are

The Ladybug Collective is a creative book-discovery home for independently published work. It brings together books published under different author names and genres without asking readers to navigate separate websites for each identity.

What we are not

We are not a traditional publishing house and do not claim to publish books on behalf of other authors.

Where this can grow

Over time, The Ladybug Collective may also spotlight and promote other independently published authors, helping readers discover new work while each author remains fully independent.
