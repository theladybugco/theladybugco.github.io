Ashes Beneath Montgomery Manor

S. E. Breazeale
Published September 8, 2026

Secrets linger in the walls of Montgomery Manor.

When the past refuses to stay buried, Elena Montgomery returns home to settle her father’s estate and uncover the truth hidden inside the decaying manor he left behind.

Old ledgers, locked rooms, and fractured beams expose a legacy of manipulation that reaches far beyond the property. Working alongside Silas, a contractor harmed by her father’s choices, Elena must confront what her family concealed—and decide what should be preserved when silence is no longer safe.
